# Income Tax & TDS Templates (India)

Practical Excel templates and checklists for TDS deduction tracking, Form 26AS/AIS reconciliation, advance tax planning, individual tax computation, and notice replies.

> **Important:** Tax rates, thresholds, section numbers and due dates change with every Finance Act, and the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act for periods from 1 April 2026 (section numbers differ). Rates in these files are **inputs / placeholders** - always verify against the law in force for the relevant year.
> All files contain **dummy data only**. Never commit real PAN, TAN, client names or figures.

## Contents
| Folder | What is inside |
|---|---|
| `tds-templates/` | TDS deduction register with due-date and late-deposit interest calculation, quarterly summary; Form 26AS/AIS vs books reconciliation |
| `income-tax-computation/` | Individual tax computation (slab table driven); advance tax instalment planner |
| `checklists/` | TDS compliance, ITR filing, tax audit (Section 44AB) |
| `notice-replies/` | Template for replying to departmental notices / intimations |
| `docs/` | How to use and publish |

## Quick start
1. Open an `.xlsx` file. Blue cells are inputs, black cells are formulas, yellow cells are key assumptions.
2. Overwrite the rows marked EXAMPLE.
3. Update `Assumptions` / `Slabs` to the rates of the year you are working on.
